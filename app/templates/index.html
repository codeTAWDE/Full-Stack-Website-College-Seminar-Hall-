Reservation Workflow

How Booking Works

A structured, conflict-free reservation workflow ensuring proper academic priority and administrative coordination.

01

Submit a Request

Faculty members log in, specify the desired date and time window, describe event objectives, and state required AV equipment.

02

Principal’s Approval

The Principal's Office reviews each application, checks schedule availability, validates academic priority, and resolves conflicts.

03

Confirmed Booking

Upon executive approval, the slot is locked on the master calendar, and automated email and in-app notifications are delivered.
